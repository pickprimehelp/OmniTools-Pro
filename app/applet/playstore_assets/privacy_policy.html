Privacy Policy for OmniTools

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

🔒 Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee: OmniTools operates with a 100% on-device, local-first architecture. All photo editing, card making, voice transformations, and calculations run strictly inside your phone's memory. No user files are ever uploaded to any cloud server.

1. Introduction

Welcome to OmniTools ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our mobile Android application in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA regulations.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

OmniTools is designed to be accessible and privacy-respecting without unnecessary barriers:

  • We do NOT require account registration, passwords, or personal profiles.
  • We do NOT collect names, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do NOT track your GPS location.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any data brokers or advertisers.

3. Permissions Used & Why

We request only the minimum Android runtime and install permissions strictly necessary to perform the features you choose to use:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively by the Voice Changer Tool to record your voice when you tap the record button. Recordings are processed locally in private app cache to apply fun sound effects (Robot, Chipmunk, Giant) and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to fetch advertising banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads provided by the official Google AdMob SDK.
  • Media Storage (Android Photo Picker): Used to let you select photos for card backgrounds or passport photos, and to save your final exported designs to your gallery.

4. Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

To provide all 40+ professional tools completely free of charge, OmniTools displays ads powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • Google AdMob may collect and process standard device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through their Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

5. In-App Donations & UPI Payments

If you choose to scan the developer donation QR code, payments are processed directly by your chosen external UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) over the NPCI banking network. OmniTools never has access to your bank account, credit card numbers, or UPI PIN.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe any child has inadvertently provided personal data, please contact us and we will promptly ensure its deletion.
