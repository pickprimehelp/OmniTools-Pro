# Privacy Policy for OmniTools

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

Welcome to **OmniTools** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, transparent experience while using our application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, device permissions, and advertising services in full compliance with **Google Play Developer Program Policies**, the General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**), the California Consumer Privacy Act (**CCPA**), and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (**COPPA**).

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

### 1.1 Local On-Device Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)
OmniTools is designed with a **privacy-first, local-offline architecture**:
- **Images & Photos:** All image processing (compression, resizing, cropping, passport photo generation, and wedding/status card rendering) is performed **entirely locally on your device**. None of your photos, images, or documents are ever uploaded to any external server or cloud database.
- **Audio & Voice Recording:** The Voice Changer tool records audio strictly into temporary, isolated local device storage using standard Android APIs. Your recorded voice is processed locally to apply pitch and speed effects. We do not store, listen to, collect, or transmit your voice recordings to any third party.
- **Documents & Financial Data:** All invoices, GST calculations, EMI formulas, notes, and PDF exports are generated strictly inside local device memory. We do not collect or view your financial transactions, invoices, or customer details.

### 1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do **NOT** require user registration, account sign-in, or passwords.
- We do **NOT** collect personal names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.
- We do **NOT** track your GPS location.
- We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade user data to any data brokers or commercial third parties.

---

## 2. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

OmniTools requests only the minimum permissions strictly necessary to deliver specific user-requested features:

1. **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)**:
   - **Purpose:** Used exclusively by the **Voice Changer Tool** to allow you to record your voice and apply audio filters (robot, chipmunk, giant, echo, etc.).
   - **Privacy Protection:** Recording only occurs when you explicitly tap the "Record" button. Audio is saved only in app-specific private storage and is never uploaded anywhere.
2. **Internet & Network State (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)**:
   - **Purpose:** Used to load official Google AdMob advertising banners, interstitials, and rewarded video ads.
3. **Media & Storage Access**:
   - **Purpose:** Used to save your generated cards, images, invoices, or PDFs to your device's Gallery/Downloads when you tap "Save".
   - **Privacy Protection:** OmniTools adheres to Google Play zero-broad-storage standards using the Android Photo Picker and standard MediaStore APIs.

OmniTools uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads) that allow us to provide all 40+ smart tools for free.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

OmniTools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly ensure no such data exists.

## 5. Security of Your Data

Because our tools operate locally on your device without transmitting files over the Internet, your designs, photos, audio clips, and documents remain private on your personal device under the security model of the Android operating system.
