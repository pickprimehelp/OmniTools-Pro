Privacy Policy for OmniTools Pro

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

OmniTools Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a utility and creative tools application by Rambeer Kashyap. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may provide while using our app.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not require users to register, create an account, or log in. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, address, or email address to any external server.

2. Local On-Device Processing

All features, including Wedding Card Maker, Status & Shayari Creator, Invoice Generator, Image Processing, PDF conversion, and Voice Changer, process data locally on your device. None of your photos, generated documents, recordings, or invoices are ever uploaded to cloud servers.

3. Permissions Used

The App requests only minimal permissions strictly necessary for its intended functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to load advertisements through Google AdMob and verify connectivity.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Used exclusively within the Voice Changer tool when you explicitly tap the record button to capture audio for local voice modulation effects. Recordings are processed locally in temporary memory and are never sent to external servers.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to keep the tools 100% free for all users. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • General approximate location data
  • Ad interaction and performance statistics

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please refer to Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
